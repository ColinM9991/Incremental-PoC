The Borrowed Moon

In the village of Bellweather, the moon disappeared every Thursday. Nobody worried; it always returned by dawn. Then one Thursday, a child named Elio found a silver coin in the well and heard a whisper from below. He lowered a lantern on a rope and saw the moon resting at the bottom, polishing its bright side against the stones. Elio dropped the coin beside it. The moon laughed, rose through the clouds, and left a trail of silver light over the rooftops.
