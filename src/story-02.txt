A Garden Above the Station

When the last train left each night, Niko climbed the stairs to platform
nine. Between the rails, he had planted mint, tomatoes, and tiny yellow
flowers. Commuters began leaving notes beside the watering can: recipes,
thank-yous, and once, a drawing of a dragon. By spring, the empty platform
had become the brightest place in the station. Niko kept one patch bare,
just in case a new traveler wanted to plant something of their own.
