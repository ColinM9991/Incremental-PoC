The Clockmaker's Window

At the edge of the harbor, Mara repaired clocks in a narrow shop with a blue door. Each morning, she wound the brass clock in the window and listened for its steady tick. One foggy Tuesday, it chimed thirteen times. A folded map slipped from behind its face, marking a lighthouse that had vanished from every chart. Mara packed her tools, followed the coast, and found its lamp still turning above the mist.
