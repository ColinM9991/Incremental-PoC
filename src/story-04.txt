The Map in the Teacup

A storm rattled the windows of the little tea shop, and a crack appeared in the oldest cup. Inside, beneath the last sip of jasmine, Lina spotted a map drawn in blue ink. It led to a hill beyond the river where a single stone bench waited beneath an apple tree. The next morning she followed it, carrying two warm buns. An old traveler was already sitting there, smiling as if he had been expecting breakfast.
